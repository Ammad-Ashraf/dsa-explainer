# Code of conduct

DSA Explainer is a learning tool, and its community should feel like a good classroom: curious, patient and kind.
We follow the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
In short:

- **Be welcoming.** People come here to learn. There are no silly questions, and everyone was a beginner once.
- **Be respectful.** Disagree with ideas, not people. No harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks.
- **Be constructive.** Explain why, suggest how, and assume good intent.
- **Respect privacy.** Do not share anyone's personal information, API keys or lesson history without consent.

Unacceptable behaviour can be reported privately to the maintainers, Ahmed Javaid and Ammad Ashraf, through their
GitHub profiles. Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers may remove comments, commits or contributions that
break this code, and may ban people who repeatedly or seriously break it, as the Contributor Covenant describes.
